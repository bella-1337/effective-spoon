Image-security monitor

Scheduled Trivy and CISA KEV scanning of the digests actually running, with private reports and Prometheus alerts

Scan scope

  • • Every running service, using the platform-specific digest
  • • Registry images are scanned remotely without the Docker socket
  • • Local or mutable images are reported unknown, not clean

Evidence

  • • Private Trivy JSON and KEV evidence (0700 / 0600)
  • • One aggregate textfile with no CVE IDs or secrets
  • • SBOMs and licence evidence are generated after each scan

Policy

  • • Scheduled mode fails only on operational errors
  • • --enforce also fails on an unexcepted KEV or fixable Critical
  • • Exceptions are time-limited and alert before expiry
